      How to play

      Five gems are hidden in a 10 × 8 mine: one red, one blue, one yellow and two white. Each gem is a tangram shape. Find the position and the orientation of each gem.

      • Tap a letter or a number on the edge to send a wave. The wave moves in a straight line. It bounces off gems. It turns 90° on a diagonal face. It goes straight back on a flat face.
      • You get the exit point and the exit colour. The colour tells you which gem colours the wave touched, in any order.
      • Ask a cell to learn its colour. This costs one turn.
      • Place all five gems and check your answer. You have two answers. Two wrong answers end the game.

      Placement rules: gem corners sit on grid points. Two gems never share an edge. Every gem is visible from at least one edge. The two white gems are not mirror images of each other.

      1v1: create a room and share the code. Each player hides five gems, then you take turns hunting in the other player's mine. Switch between "Their mine" and "Your mine" to see your questions or theirs. The first correct answer wins. A wrong check costs one of your two checks. Give up and your opponent wins. You do not need to be online at the same time: close the game, come back later, open the link and tap Join. Anyone with the room code can read the room, so keep the code between you.

      Turn alerts: in the room card, tap Alerts and allow notifications. Your phone then gets a notification after each of your opponent's moves and messages, even with the game closed. On iPhone this works for the home-screen version only. Without push alerts, the ntfy app route still works: subscribe to a topic name of your own in the free ntfy app and type it in the 1v1 screen.
